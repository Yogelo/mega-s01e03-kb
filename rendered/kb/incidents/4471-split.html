The three problems

parser — day and month swapped. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 3 May becomes 5 March. This is the fix that is queued. Ticket #1.

hint — the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. Under its date field, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the tenant’s way, day first. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it: the form needs a day-first hint. The table marks it “no ticket of its own”; it has one now: #2.

one-day move — the date lands one day off. These reports describe a date one day early, an invite sent before the booking, and two clients in Lisbon and Toronto who got different days for one meeting. That is a time zone problem, not a swap. The desk noted them (desk-notes.json). Ticket #3.
