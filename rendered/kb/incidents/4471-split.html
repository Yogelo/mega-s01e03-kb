The three problems

parser — day and month swapped. The shared parser read every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 3 May became 5 March. Engineering fixed it when #4471 closed. Ticket #1, closed.

hint — the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. Under its date field, booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY. People typed month first, as the hint told them, and the form read the tenant’s way, day first. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix did not touch it; the form is retired instead, so no fix is coming. Ticket #2, closed as won’t fix.

one-day move — the date lands one day off. These reports describe a date one day early, an invite sent before the booking, and two clients in Lisbon and Toronto who got different days for one meeting. That is a time zone problem, not a swap. The desk noted them (desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant, so the table counts them there. The parser fix does not close them. Ticket #3, open.
